New to building things with code? Good. You don’t need any experience, only curiosity. Every lesson is written like a recipe card, so you always know what you’ll make, what you need, and how to check that it worked.

## Why adobo?

Adobo is one of the most loved Filipino dishes, and every family makes it a little differently. A simple version needs just ten ingredients: chicken, soy sauce, vinegar, black pepper, sugar, bay leaf, garlic, onion, water and oil. Building a website is the same kind of thing: a few simple ingredients, some heat, and a lot of tasting and adjusting. So each lesson compares the tricky ideas to cooking, and then you make it your own.

## The kitchen map

:::map In the kitchen | On the website
- The ingredients on the counter | **HTML**: the content and structure of a page
- The seasoning: soy sauce, vinegar, garlic | **CSS or Tailwind**: how the page looks
- The heat that makes it cook | **JavaScript or Alpine.js**: what makes the page react
- Tasting and adjusting as you go | **Testing and fixing**: try it, look, change it
- Your recipe notebook | **Git**: saving each version, like “adobo v2, less vinegar”
- The kitchen behind the scenes | **A backend like Laravel**: the part visitors never see
:::
