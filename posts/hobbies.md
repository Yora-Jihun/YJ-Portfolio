## Programming

I love to code, because I can do weird things that I like to do with programming languages. It’s not just about solving problems, it’s about bringing ideas to life and creating tools that make a difference.

## Sports

I love playing every kind of sports like football, volleyball, badminton, and I want also to try tennis, and to learn how to swim. Also my main sport is basketball, I’m not saying I’m a professional player but if you want to challenge me it’s fine.

## Studying

I love studying in my own way because I want to expand my knowledge. Reading and watching videos is my source of learning and after that I apply it by myself and creating solutions and making my own experiments for me to be able to understand everything.

## Writing

I’m not a writer but I love to write in essay form. English or Tagalog is my language when I’m writing essays. I also write any kind of story about life or motivational and inspirational quotes. I learned the English language when I started to write essays in a form of English.

## Music

I love music because it makes me calm and more active in working or doing a project like a website. I also love dancing and singing but I’m shy doing it in public, but when it comes to presentation in school or a trip, of course I’m doing it for grades and for fun.

## Life Goals

In life my goal is to become successful in everything I put in efforts. I love teaching other people and my friends. I also give them an inspirational story and motivation for them to achieve what they really want to achieve in life. I want to take advance in everything and care about the future at an early age.

## Cooking

I was in grade 2 level when I started to cook, only fried dishes. When I got bigger and bigger I started to learn how to set ingredients. I learned it by watching some chefs on YouTube like Chinese, Korean, Japanese and so much more. I also like turning Filipino dishes into an extraordinary dish by adjusting the taste and enhancing the flavor of it. I’m not a chef but I love to experiment with food and to learn how to cook perfectly.

## Workout

Staying fit and healthy is important to me. Working out helps me maintain energy and focus, whether I’m coding, playing sports, or tackling daily challenges. It’s about building strength both physically and mentally.

## Watching Videos

I love watching movies on Netflix like sci-fi, horror, thriller, love story, comedy, those are my favorite genres to watch. I also like watching anime sometimes and after 3 episodes I feel sleepy. Documentary, history and learning videos are also my favorites to watch. Just like I said, ‘I keep expanding my knowledge.’

I also love creating logos and other stuff like logos for gaming and also for video editing when it comes to gaming or events. I’m interested in making montage videos of it.
